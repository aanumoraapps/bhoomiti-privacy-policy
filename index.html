BhooMiti Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026 App package: com.mora.bhoomiti App name: BhooMiti

This Privacy Policy applies to BhooMiti, an Android land measurement app. It explains how the app handles information when you use its measurement, conversion, drawing, PDF/text sharing, backup and restore features.

Based on the current Android project, BhooMiti works offline and does not include Firebase, Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, advertising SDKs, account login or cloud sync.

Information the App Stores

BhooMiti stores information that you enter or explicitly save on your device, including:

  • land measurements and calculation results;
  • regional unit profiles such as katha, dhur, bigha and dismil settings;
  • saved plots, Chauhaddi details, drawings, map records and PDF/text output details;
  • local backup and restore records created by the app;
  • app preferences such as language, theme and default units.

How Information Is Used

The app uses your entered information to provide its local features: land area calculation, unit conversion, land price estimation, Chauhaddi drawing, map drawing, PDF creation, text sharing, saved plot history and backup/restore.

Permissions and Device Access

The current app manifest does not declare runtime permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone, calendar or account access. The app uses Android system features such as file sharing, file opening and document creation only when you choose actions like export, share, open PDF, backup or restore.

Advertising, Analytics and Crash Reporting

The current release does not include advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics or similar tracking/crash-reporting services. The app does not automatically send usage analytics or saved land records to the developer.

Third-Party Services and SDKs

The app uses Android and AndroidX libraries to provide app functionality, local storage, user interface and file sharing. No third-party service is used to collect or upload user data in the current release. If you share a PDF, text report or backup using Android's share sheet, the app you select, such as a messaging, email or file app, handles that shared content under its own privacy policy.

Data Sharing

BhooMiti does not automatically share your data. Data is shared only when you choose an export, share, save, backup or restore action. You control the destination selected through Android's system UI.

Data Security

Your saved app data is stored locally on your device. Release builds are configured to disable debug logging and cleartext HTTP. No method of local storage or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so you should keep backup files in a safe place and share exported documents only with people or apps you trust.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved records remain on your device until you delete them in the app, uninstall the app or clear app storage. Backup files remain wherever you save or share them and must be deleted separately from that location if you no longer need them.

Children's Privacy

BhooMiti is a utility app for land measurement and land-record related work. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Calculation Limitation

BhooMiti calculates from the measurements and method you enter. Results are guidance for understanding land area and related values. The app does not determine ownership, legal boundary rights or replace an official field survey.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features change, for example if account sync, cloud backup, analytics, advertising or other online services are added in a future release. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
